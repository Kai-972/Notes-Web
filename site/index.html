how it works

Swipe between three pages.

No tabs and no menus to dig through. Today is where you tick things off, Notes is where you write, Review is where you look back.

the day line

Your whole day on one line.

It runs from 06:00 to 02:00. Notes you stamp, tasks with a time and focus sessions all land on it, and a glowing dot shows where you are now.
