installing

  1. 1Download the APK on your phone.
  2. 2Open it. Android asks to allow installs from your browser or files app. Allow it once.
  3. 3Open notes, pick a few goals and habits, and you are on Today.
  4. 4For reminders on time, allow Notifications and Alarms & reminders when you turn the first one on.

Updating later: install the new APK over the old one and your data stays. Export a backup first anyway.
